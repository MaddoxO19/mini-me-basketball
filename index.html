What later systems can now rely on
Every franchise has a stable id, name, shortName, conference, division, sport, founded year, offensive rating, defensive rating, pace and derived OVR. Standings are keyed by franchise id and derive conference seed independently from division presentation. No correction layer has been added to the Phase 1 engine.
