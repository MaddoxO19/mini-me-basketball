Lifecycle Audit

Phase 6E integrity checks
Newly drafted rookies are not aged or developed again in the same offseason. Existing players age one year. Young players progress toward POT, prime-age players are broadly stable, veterans decline progressively, and retirement probability rises materially from the mid-30s.
