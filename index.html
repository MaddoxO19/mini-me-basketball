Lifecycle Audit

Phase 6E integrity checks
Newly drafted rookies are not aged or developed again in the same offseason. Existing players age one year. Young players progress toward POT, prime-age players are broadly stable, veterans decline progressively, and retirement probability rises materially from the mid-30s. Contract-year decrement and permanent season rollover remain deliberately deferred to the next offseason phase.
Phase 6F

From one season to the next.

The validated postseason, draft and lifecycle systems now connect into a permanent career rollover. Contracts advance, expiring players enter free agency, rosters are brought back inside league limits, future draft assets roll forward and a fresh 82-game schedule is created for the new season.

Phase 4

The Play-In Tournament.

Once all 82 regular-season games are complete, seeds 7 through 10 in each conference enter the play-in. The 7 v 8 winner claims seed 7. The 9 v 10 loser is eliminated. The remaining two teams then play for seed 8.
